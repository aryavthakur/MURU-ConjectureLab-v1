## Summary count

**Methodology note (corrected after hostile review):** each of the 25 distinct repair-class rows above is tallied under exactly *one* Classification-column value — its primary disposition — even where the row's basis text also names a stronger, nested claim (e.g. "M1 statistic redesign" is tallied once under `CLOSED_NEGATIVE`, with its additional `CLOSED_THEORETICALLY_IMPOSSIBLE` sub-claim for the common-amplitude component noted in-row but not separately counted). An earlier draft of this document double-counted three such rows and separately double-booked "set-valued retention" into both `REMAINS_LICENSED_FOR_TEST` and `WAITING_ON_E2`, producing an internally-inconsistent tally (a printed sum of 28, a row count of 25, and prose saying "~24"). The counts below are the single-classification-per-row tally, verified to sum to the actual row count.
