## Dropped candidates and why

The retention rule, applied before any outcome was observed: computable
for every compound, non-degenerate, mechanistically motivated, and not
rank-redundant with a descriptor already retained.

| Dropped | Reason |
|---|---|
| `exact_mass`, `mol_wt` | Spearman **1.000** with `precursor_mz` — rank-identical and mathematically dependent (`precursor_mz = exact_mass + 1.00728`) |
| `labute_asa` | 0.985 with mass, 0.993 with heavy-atom count. The master plan's 'molecular volume / CCS proxy' slot carries nothing beyond mass at this resolution and would contaminate the K5 mass ablation |
| `heavy_atom_count` | 0.974 with mass, 0.903 with `total_atom_count`, which is the principled 3N−6 proxy |

**Descriptors were not added later because cross-validation performance
was disappointing.** The list is the one frozen in the pre-registration.

importance is reported over correlated blocks. Prediction is unaffected by
collinearity; interpretation is.

### Mass block for the K5 ablation

`precursor_mz`, `total_atom_count`, `rdbe` are removed **together**.
Dropping the named mass variable alone would leave its proxies in place
and understate how much of the effect mass carries.
