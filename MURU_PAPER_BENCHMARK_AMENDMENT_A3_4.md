### Reason for Amendment
A hostile scientific and mathematical audit of Amendment A3.3 (`audit/MURU_A3_3_MATHEMATICAL_REVIEW.md`, commit `78cc7c2`) confirmed the mathematical accuracy and algebraic invariance of A3.3's Parameter Recovery derivative operators, but identified a critical domain-conformance defect in its Predictive Equivalence specification:
1. **Predictive Domain Breakdown:** The independent Cartesian Sobol hypercube ($\text{mass} \in [100, 800]$, non-mass covariates $\in [-2.5, 2.5]$) departs from the synthetic data-generating distribution. For Family F09 (`mass_saturating_descriptor`), setting $\text{descriptor} \in [-2.5, 2.5]$ encounters a division-by-zero pole/singularity at $\text{descriptor} = -1.0$ and generates negative truth values in $[-1.0, -0.72]$, neither of which exists in the generator. Furthermore, independent uniform sampling destroys the $r \approx 0.98$ collinearity between `descriptor` and `correlated_distractor` and samples $96\%$ of points outside the generator's $[0, 1]^2$ descriptor support.
2. **Terminology Precision:** Describing $c_{\text{desc}}$ as "coordinate-free" is too strong; while scale-invariant to $g$ and invariant to candidate factoring, $c_{\text{desc}}$ has physical dimension $[\text{descriptor}]^{-1}$ and depends on the descriptor coordinate.
3. **Machine-Readable Formalization:** The relative RMSE formula and zero-variance correlation failure semantics require explicit, unambiguous machine-readable definitions.
4. **Decomposition Reporting:** Component reporting for $p_{\text{mass}} / 156$ and $c_{\text{desc}} / 84$ should be formally specified.

Amendment A3.4 provides the prospective pre-outcome repair for these items before any Development rerun, before structural-null calibration is executed, and before the sealed Held-out partition is opened.

## A3.4.1 Parameter Recovery Scientific Contract

### Scientific Role & Denominator Discipline
- **Role:** SECONDARY descriptive endpoint (never part of G1, G2, or G3).
- **Applicable Families:** F01–F05, F07–F12, F17, F18 (13 families $\times$ 12 held-out cases = **156 cases**).
- **Denominator Discipline:** Strictly fixed at **156**. Unresolved, non-finite, missing, or unparseable candidate expressions count as non-successes and never reduce the denominator.

### Invariance Properties: Scale-Invariant and Algebraically Invariant
In the M0 collapse model ($\mu(E) \approx \Phi(E/g)$), the overall multiplicative scale of $g$ is non-identifiable. The derivative operators normalize by $\hat{g}(\mathbf{x}_0)$, ensuring that the extracted parameters are **scale-invariant** (invariant to any global positive multiplier $A > 0$) and **algebraically invariant** (invariant to factoring, expansion, or mass reference shifts such as $\sqrt{m/250}$ vs $\sqrt{m}$) within the frozen coordinate system of the benchmark.

### Canonical Reference Anchor Point
All derivatives are evaluated at the frozen reference anchor:
$$\mathbf{x}_0 = (\text{mass} = 250.0, \text{descriptor} = 0.0, \text{descriptor2} = 0.0, \text{distractor} = 0.0, \text{correlated\_distractor} = 0.0)$$

At $\mathbf{x}_0$, all chemical modulations in the generative laws evaluate to exactly $1.0$, which isolates the logarithmic mass elasticity from chemical modulations and enables clean extraction of the linear descriptor coupling.

### Identifiable Parameter Definitions

1. **Mass Scaling Exponent ($p_{\text{mass}}$):**
   Applicable to all 156 cases. Defined as the dimensionless logarithmic elasticity of $\hat{g}$ with respect to mass at $\mathbf{x}_0$:
   $$p_{\text{mass}}(\hat{g}) = \left. \frac{\partial \ln \hat{g}}{\partial \ln \text{mass}} \right|_{\mathbf{x}_0} = \left. \frac{\text{mass}}{\hat{g}} \frac{\partial \hat{g}}{\partial \text{mass}} \right|_{\mathbf{x}_0}$$
   - **Planted Truth ($p_{\text{truth}}$):** `truth.exponents["mass"]` ($0.50$ for F01–F05, F08–F12, F17, F18; drawn from $[0.45, 0.75]$ for F07).
   - **Absolute Tolerance:** $|p_{\text{mass}}(\hat{g}) - p_{\text{truth}}| \le 0.15$.

2. **Normalized Descriptor Coupling Coefficient ($c_{\text{desc}}$):**
   Applicable to the 84 held-out cases in descriptor-dependent families (F08, F09, F10, F11, F12, F17, F18). Defined as the relative sensitivity of $\hat{g}$ to the active descriptor(s) at $\mathbf{x}_0$:
   - For linear/affine (`mass_affine_descriptor`, F08, F11, F12, F17) and saturating (`mass_saturating_descriptor`, F09):
     $$c_{\text{desc}}(\hat{g}) = \left. \frac{1}{\hat{g}} \frac{\partial \hat{g}}{\partial \text{descriptor}} \right|_{\mathbf{x}_0}$$
   - For interaction (`mass_interaction`, F10):
     $$c_{\text{desc}}(\hat{g}) = \left. \frac{1}{\hat{g}} \frac{\partial^2 \hat{g}}{\partial \text{descriptor} \partial \text{descriptor2}} \right|_{\mathbf{x}_0}$$
   - For exponential (`mass_exponential_descriptor`, F18, where generative law is $\exp(c \cdot d / 3)$):
     $$c_{\text{desc}}(\hat{g}) = \left. \frac{3}{\hat{g}} \frac{\partial \hat{g}}{\partial \text{descriptor}} \right|_{\mathbf{x}_0}$$
   - **Planted Truth ($c_{\text{truth}}$):** `truth.coefficients["coefficient"]` (drawn from $[0.25, 0.55]$).
   - **Absolute Tolerance:** $|c_{\text{desc}}(\hat{g}) - c_{\text{truth}}| \le 0.10$.

For mass-only families (F01–F05, F07; 72 cases), only $p_{\text{mass}}$ is evaluated.

### Per-Case Success Rule & Reporting Decomposition
A case achieves **Parameter Recovery Success** if and only if:
1. A candidate expression $\hat{g}$ is parsed under the frozen symbolic grammar.
2. $\hat{g}(\mathbf{x}_0)$ evaluates to a strictly positive finite real number ($\hat{g}(\mathbf{x}_0) > 0$).
3. The symbolic derivatives for all applicable parameters at $\mathbf{x}_0$ exist, are finite, and satisfy:
   $$|p_{\text{mass}}(\hat{g}) - p_{\text{truth}}| \le 0.15$$
   and (for the 84 descriptor-applicable cases):
   $$|c_{\text{desc}}(\hat{g}) - c_{\text{truth}}| \le 0.10$$

**Reporting Contract:**
The final paper benchmark report shall prospectively present:
- **Joint Parameter Recovery Rate:** $N_{\text{joint\_success}} / 156$ (with Wilson 95% CI) [Governing secondary endpoint].
- **Mass Exponent Recovery Rate:** $N_{p\text{\_success}} / 156$ (with Wilson 95% CI) [Descriptive decomposition].
- **Descriptor Coupling Recovery Rate:** $N_{c\text{\_success}} / 84$ (with Wilson 95% CI) [Descriptive decomposition].

### Evaluation Metrics and Failure Rules

For each case, let $\mathbf{y}_{\text{true}} \in \mathbb{R}^{2160}$ be the case-specific true law $g_{\text{true}}(\mathbf{x}_i)$ evaluated across the reference rows, and let $\hat{\mathbf{y}} \in \mathbb{R}^{2160}$ be the discovered candidate expression $\hat{g}(\mathbf{x}_i)$ evaluated across the same rows.

1. **Valid Point Set ($\mathcal{V}$):**
   $$\mathcal{V} = \{i \in \{1, \dots, 2160\} \mid \hat{y}_i \text{ is finite and } \hat{y}_i > 0, \text{ and } y_{\text{true}, i} \text{ is finite and } y_{\text{true}, i} > 0\}$$
   - **Validity Threshold:** $\text{valid\_fraction} = \frac{|\mathcal{V}|}{2160} \ge 0.995$ (at most 10 invalid points out of 2,160).
   - If $\text{valid\_fraction} < 0.995 \to$ **Predictive Equivalence Failure**.

2. **Optimal Multiplicative Scale Alignment ($c^*$):**
   Because $g$ is identified up to global positive scale in the collapse model, $\hat{\mathbf{y}}$ is aligned to $\mathbf{y}_{\text{true}}$ via least-squares scalar multiplier computed strictly over $\mathcal{V}$:
   $$c^* = \frac{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}} y_{\text{true}, i} \cdot \hat{y}_i}{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}} \hat{y}_i^2}$$
   - **Constraint:** $c^*$ must exist, be finite, and satisfy $c^* > 0$.
   - If $\sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}} \hat{y}_i^2 = 0$, $c^*$ is non-finite, or $c^* \le 0 \to$ **Predictive Equivalence Failure**.
   - Affine intercept shifts ($a + b \hat{g}$ with $a \ne 0$) and coefficient refitting are strictly **FORBIDDEN**.

3. **Scale-Adjusted Relative RMSE:**
   $$\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{|\mathcal{V}|} \sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}} (c^* \hat{y}_i - y_{\text{true}, i})^2}$$
   $$\text{TRUTH\_RMS} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{|\mathcal{V}|} \sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}} y_{\text{true}, i}^2}$$
   $$\text{REL\_RMSE} = \frac{\text{RMSE}}{\text{TRUTH\_RMS}}$$
   - **Constraint:** $\text{TRUTH\_RMS}$ must be finite and $> 0$.
   - **Success Threshold:** $\text{REL\_RMSE} \le 0.05$ (at most 5% relative root-mean-square error).

4. **Pearson Correlation ($r$):**
   $$r = \frac{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}} (y_{\text{true}, i} - \bar{y}_{\text{true}})(\hat{y}_i - \bar{\hat{y}})}{\sqrt{\sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}} (y_{\text{true}, i} - \bar{y}_{\text{true}})^2 \sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}} (\hat{y}_i - \bar{\hat{y}})^2}}$$
   - **Zero-Variance Rule:** If either $\hat{\mathbf{y}}_{\mathcal{V}}$ or $\mathbf{y}_{\text{true}, \mathcal{V}}$ has zero sample variance (e.g. a constant model $\hat{g} = 1.0$), $r$ is formally defined as $0.0 \to$ **Predictive Equivalence Failure** (fails $r \ge 0.990$).
   - **Success Threshold:** $r \ge 0.990$.

### Complete Per-Case Predictive Equivalence Success Rule
A case achieves **Predictive Equivalence Success** if and only if ALL seven conditions hold:
1. Candidate expression $\hat{g}$ is present and parseable under the frozen symbolic grammar.
2. $\text{valid\_fraction} \ge 0.995$ ($|\mathcal{V}| \ge 2150$).
3. Scale factor $c^*$ exists, is finite, and satisfies $c^* > 0$.
4. $\text{TRUTH\_RMS}$ exists, is finite, and satisfies $\text{TRUTH\_RMS} > 0$.
5. $\text{REL\_RMSE} \le 0.05$.
6. Both $\hat{\mathbf{y}}_{\mathcal{V}}$ and $\mathbf{y}_{\text{true}, \mathcal{V}}$ have non-zero sample variance.
7. Pearson correlation $r \ge 0.990$.
