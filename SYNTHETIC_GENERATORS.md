## Covariate source

Real-covariate, synthetic-response worlds are built on the **439 development
compounds** and the frozen energy grid {15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90} NCE, so
mass-descriptor correlation, descriptor ranges, scaffold grouping and chemical
support stay realistic. **Only covariates are read.** No observed `mu` value
enters any synthetic response; `tests/test_p3_seal.py` asserts that the
generated response is not the observed one. The 110 sealed confirmation
compounds are excluded by connectivity key before any covariate is touched.

`GA` is fully synthetic and uses no real chemistry, guarding against the
possibility that recovery depends on some property of the real descriptor
matrix.

## Shared trajectory shape

`Φ(u) = 0.2414 + 0.7586 / (1 + u^1.4874)`, a logistic in log-energy, with
`mu = Φ(E / g(z))`. The three constants were calibrated **once**, before any
governed run, against the marginal `mu`-by-energy means of the development
corpus and reproduce those six means to within 0.011. They are frozen.

## Determinism and versioning

Every world records generator version, truth version, seed, parameter set,
covariate source, planted equation, noise regime, split assignment and an
output SHA-256 over the response, the energy column, the compound keys and the
descriptor matrix. World seeds derive from
`sha256(generator_version | family | replicate | noise_regime)`, so a clean
rerun of any single world reproduces it without replaying the ones before it.
`tests/test_p3_generators.py` asserts hash equality across rebuilds and hash
inequality across replicates and noise regimes.

Generated outputs are never edited by hand.

## Noise regimes

| Regime | SD on `mu` | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `low` | 0.01 | below any measured variability |
| `moderate` | **0.0295** | the Phase 1 conservative inter-mixture variability estimate |
| `adverse` | 0.06 | roughly double the upper bound |

`0.0295` is an **upper bound on technical repeatability**, not an instrument
noise floor and not pure Gaussian technical error
(`MASTER_PLAN_CLARIFICATIONS.md` C4). It is used only as a scale reference.

Missing cells are applied at the **measured** rate
0.0097 (99.03% coverage in the real corpus), never taking a
compound below 5 energies. The master plan's assumed
15% missingness does not describe this corpus (`DEVIATIONS_P3.md` D3).

## G4 — the pure null the K6 gate rests on

Across the 100 G4 worlds, the largest absolute correlation between the
compound-level offset and **any** descriptor is **0.1613**. The null
contains no descriptor signal to find.

## GC — the Phase 2 measurement-coupling adversary

Phase 2 restriction 1 is binding. GC holds fractional fragmentation fixed as a
**law** — every compound draws fragment positions and intensities from one
common, mass-independent distribution — lets precursor mass vary, applies a
fixed absolute low-mass cutoff, and computes `mu` exactly as the real pipeline
does: `mu = SY + (1 − SY)·φ` with `φ = ⟨m/z fragment⟩ / m/z precursor`.
Per-compound realizations vary but are **independent of every descriptor**, so
any descriptor structure found here is spurious by construction.

**Restriction, restated because it binds.** This generator tests robustness of
the discovery system. It does **not** identify the mechanism of the real
association, and it does not establish what fraction of the observed
association is artifactual. No such fraction is claimed anywhere in Phase 3.

## GRT — retention-time surrogate

A latent property drives both the descriptors and retention time; RT predicts
the response observationally but is not the planted cause. This is a bounded
stress test, not chromatography modelling, and it does **not** resolve the real
NC7 finding causally. Phase 2's wording stands: RT carries predictive signal by
itself but adds little beyond Tier A descriptors, consistent with a
structure-associated surrogate in this dataset; independent confounding cannot
be completely excluded.
