## This is not a ceiling

The master plan calls B7 the 'predictability ceiling' and states that
'any baseline beating B7 signals a bug'. Both are too strong, and
`MASTER_PLAN_CLARIFICATIONS.md` C2 revokes them.

A gradient-boosted tree ensemble under nested grouped CV is **one model
family with one frozen search space**. It is not an information-theoretic
upper bound on recoverable information, and it is not guaranteed to beat
simpler models — trees are weak at smooth extrapolation along a continuous
axis, which is exactly what `mu ~ f(energy, mass)` is. Treating a loss as
a defect would create pressure to tune until it wins, which is the
researcher degree of freedom the pre-registration exists to remove.

**The benchmark was not re-tuned after seeing its results, and its search
space was not enlarged.**

| Selection | inner 4-fold grouped CV only; the outer fold never informs selection |

The grid and the fingerprint floor were reduced from the originally frozen
values for compute reasons, before any performance was observed
(`DEVIATIONS.md` D12). Both reductions can only **weaken** the benchmark,
so they make K4A and K4B **harder** to pass — the conservative direction.
