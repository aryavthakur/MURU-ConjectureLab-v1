# MURU Phase 3 project report

Phase 3 produced a complete internally drafted manuscript, Word and PDF versions, five figures, machine-readable result tables, supporting information, a verified 26-source citation package, technical verification reports, three simulated adversarial reviews, and a tested statistical reproduction archive. The external primary analysis was rerun from a detached worktree at the frozen release and matched the committed result exactly, including population counts, P1 values, scaffold-bootstrap interval and novelty-bin ratios.

The paper's conclusion is deliberately limited: MURU-WUR-v2 showed a 2.93% lower pooled fragmentation-extent RMSE than the Tier A reference on the external population, but did not meet the 5% practical criterion. Tail risk did not improve, and the least-similar structural quartile had slightly worse candidate performance. The fixed energy map is computationally reproducible but not established as a physical cross-instrument calibration; CE Design A remains unresolved.

The raw mzML-to-model pipeline was not rerun because the original MSnLib archives are third-party data and are not bundled. The reviewer kit performs frozen-output statistical reconstruction. The absent Phase 2 audit archive prevented verification of the max-T development interval and four-frame census defect. These are documented as specific outstanding evidence gaps rather than filled with assumptions.

The master manuscript is 13 pages (standard citation version), the online-citation rendering is 17 pages, and supporting information is four pages. Both reviewer and complete deliverable ZIPs passed archive integrity checks; the reviewer archive was run successfully after fresh extraction.

No journal submission occurred. No human peer review, coauthor approval or legal clearance is claimed. See `release/PUBLICATION_READINESS.md` and `release/REMAINING_HUMAN_DECISIONS.md` for final status.
