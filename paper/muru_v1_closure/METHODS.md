# MURU ConjectureLab v1 — Methods

Manuscript-ready Methods for the synthetic benchmark. Every number cited here is generated by
`scripts/build_muru_v1_manuscript_tables.py` from the committed analysis artifacts; none is
transcribed by hand.

## 1. Scope and claim boundary

**This study evaluates machinery on synthetic data. It makes no claim about real collision-induced
dissociation chemistry.**

Every case in this benchmark is generated from a known planted law by a frozen generator. No
measured mass spectrum, no real compound, and no observed fragmentation intensity enters any case,
any fit, any endpoint or any gate. The benchmark therefore measures whether a symbolic-discovery
pipeline can recover structure that is *known to be present by construction*, and whether it
correctly refuses structure that is *known to be absent by construction*.

Results in this study are statements about the pipeline's behaviour on that synthetic population.
They are not statements about CID chemistry, about any real analyte, or about the pipeline's
performance on measured data. A separate real-data **Confirmation** partition exists and remains
sealed and unopened; nothing here licenses any inference about it.

## 2. Benchmark construction

### 2.1 Case families

Twenty families (F01–F20) were defined prospectively, each posing one scientific question. Families
divide into four groups:

- **Recoverable-structure families** (F01, F02, F03, F04, F05, F08, F09, F10, F11, F12, F17, F18) —
  a scalar collapse and a symbolic law are both planted, under varying noise, missingness, boundary
  proximity, distractor structure and algebraic difficulty. These carry both the scalar and the
  symbolic endpoints.
- **Adequacy-probe families** (F06, F13, F14, F15, F16) — the scalar model M0 is deliberately
  violated (horizontal shape, high-energy vertical, low-energy vertical, or combined). These carry
  **no primary endpoint**; they exist to test whether the adequacy ladder detects the violation.
- **Mass-only family** (F07) — a mass-only law is planted; accepting richer non-mass structure is a
  safety failure. Carries the scalar endpoint and the safety endpoint, but **not** family recovery.
- **Null and adversarial families** (F19, F20) — F19 cycles three variants that destroy the
  descriptor link, preserve mass only, or destroy the trajectory; F20 cycles a latent driver, a
  measurement coupling, and an out-of-grammar trap. These carry the safety endpoint. F19A and F19B
  additionally carry the scalar endpoint; F19C and all F20 variants do not.

Families F19 and F20 cycle variants across replicates (`F19A→F19B→F19C`, `F20A→F20B→F20C`); all
other families have a single `BASE` variant whose code is the family code.

### 2.2 Partitions

| Partition | Cases per family | Total cases | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Development | 4 | 80 | executed; used for development |
| **Held-out** | **12** | **240** | **executed once, sealed, analysed here** |
| Challenge | 3 | 60 | **not opened** |
| Confirmation (real data) | — | — | **sealed, never opened** |

Held-out is the primary evaluation partition. It was executed exactly once, sealed
cryptographically before any analysis, and never rerun.

### 2.3 Search protocol

Each case was searched with **30 independent seeds**, giving **7,200 searches** over the 240
Held-out cases. Seeds are derived deterministically per case by `rc5_seeds.case_search_seeds` from a
frozen band disjoint from every other partition's band; injectivity was verified across all 11,400
seeds spanning all 380 cases. The symbolic engine is PySR 1.5.10 over a frozen grammar with frozen
search settings, both digest-pinned in the execution manifest.

## 3. Endpoints

Three primary endpoints were frozen prospectively. **Each has its own denominator, derived from the
registry by summing per-family applicability over the Held-out replicates. None of them is 240.**

| Endpoint | Registry name | Role | Denominator | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** scalar competence | `scalar_competence` | primary scalar | **164** | Wilson lower 95% ≥ 0.70 |
| **G2** family recovery | `family_recovery` | primary symbolic | **144** | Wilson lower 95% ≥ 0.70 |
| **G3** structural safety | `principal_structural_safety` | primary safety | **36** | Wilson upper 95% ≤ 0.15 |

164 + 144 + 36 = 344 ≠ 240. The endpoints **overlap** — a case may serve both G1 and G2 — and the
five adequacy-probe families carry none of them. Any construction that scores an endpoint over all
240 case records is definitionally wrong, and the frozen scorers raise on a sequence of the wrong
length precisely to prevent it.

Denominators are **fixed**. An unevaluable case never leaves its denominator: under G1 and G2 it
receives no credit and costs exactly what a failure costs; under G3 it counts as a **violation**.
That conservatism is what makes the endpoints honest, and it is load-bearing in the results.

### 3.1 G1 — scalar competence

A case is *scalar competent* iff all three conjuncts hold:

1. `g_spearman ≥ 0.80` — Spearman rank correlation between the fold-local estimated `g` and the true
   `log g` over the 30 test compounds;
2. `trajectory_mae ≤ 0.80 · per_energy_mean_mae` — a skill score against a training-only
   per-energy-mean baseline, both evaluated over the identical observed cell set;
3. `m0_accepted` — the A1 adequacy ladder did not reject M0.

The estimator is A1's M0 under within-compound leave-one-energy-out, fitted with A1's frozen
protocol (`log_g ∈ [-2, 2]`, 81-point grid, 3 × 21 refinement, shrink 10). It is **not** the
discovered symbolic expression.

### 3.2 G2 — family recovery

A case is a G2 *success* iff `support_status == MATCH` **AND** `family_status == MATCH`. Unresolved
support, or an unresolved or ambiguous family, yields UNEVALUABLE. Anything else is a failure. The
conjunction is essential: recovering the right variables with the wrong functional form is not
recovery.

### 3.3 G3 — principal structural safety

G3 counts **violations**, and its gate is an **upper** bound: low is good. The frozen dispatcher
`g3_contract.classify_g3_event` is the sole authority. Per variant:

- `UNEVALUABLE` acceptance ⇒ **VIOLATION**, for every variant, conservatively.
- A case the pipeline *rejected* ⇒ SAFE. Declining is never a safety failure.
- An *accepted* case ⇒ SAFE only if its effective support is permitted for that variant: mass-only
  for F07, F19A and F19B; nothing at all for F19C, F20A, F20B and F20C. Otherwise **UNSAFE**.

## 4. The structural-acceptance predicate

Acceptance is **truth-blind**: planted truth is projected out before the predicate runs and cannot
reach it even by accident. The predicate is ordered and short-circuiting; a case's terminal
`gate_reached` records where it stopped.

| # | Gate | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A1 adequacy | only `M0_NOT_REJECTED` proceeds |
| — | candidate present | else UNEVALUABLE at `no_candidate` |
| 2 | null threshold | `valid_r2 > null_threshold[min(complexity, 20)]` |
| 3 | stability | `selection_fraction ≥ 20/30` |
| 4 | complexity | `complexity ≤ 20` |
| 5 | invalid fraction | `invalid_fraction ≤ 0.005` |
| 6 | effective support | non-empty |
| **7** | **ceiling** | `ceiling_fraction ≥ 0.80` **OR** (`ceiling_r2 < 0.05` **AND** `candidate_test_r2 > null_threshold[·]`) |
| **8** | **hard falsification** | all four hard rungs PASS, fail-closed |

The null threshold table is the frozen A3.2 structural-null calibration, digest-pinned on every
record.

**Gate 7 is the ceiling comparison at position 7.** It is not structural acceptance, and it is
unrelated to the rung `F7_INFLUENCE_DROP` — the two share a digit by coincidence. The waiver branch
exists because when the estimated ceiling is near zero the ceiling *ratio* is uninformative; the
waiver substitutes a raw candidate R² floor. That floor is F5's one non-redundant contribution,
folded into this branch and appearing exactly once in the whole predicate.

**Gate 8 is `check_gate8` over exactly four hard rungs**: `F1_REPRODUCIBILITY`,
`F4_COMPOUND_HOLDOUT`, `F7_INFLUENCE_DROP`, `F10_NEGATIVE_CONTROL`. It is **fail-closed**: the
predicate is `result != PASS`, so a missing rung, an explicit FAIL, and a stray `NOT_APPLICABLE` all
reject identically. **G1 is an endpoint, not a gate, and is not a conjunct of Gate 8.**

`STRUCTURAL_ACCEPTED` means gates 1–8 all passed. It is an **acceptance count, not an endpoint
rate**; it has no frozen denominator and must never be reported over 240.

### 4.1 Falsification rung roles

| Rung | Role | Gating |
|---|---|---|
| F1_REPRODUCIBILITY | hard gate | yes |
| F4_COMPOUND_HOLDOUT | hard gate | yes |
| F5_SCAFFOLD_HOLDOUT | **superseded**; floor folded into the Gate 7 waiver branch | no |
| F7_INFLUENCE_DROP | hard gate | yes |
| F9_ENERGY_SUBSET | secondary reported stress test | **no** |
| F10_NEGATIVE_CONTROL | hard gate | yes |

F9 is computed for every case reaching Gate 8 and recorded as `f9_stress_test_result` and
`f9_stress_test_metric`. Its acceptance calibration status is permanently
`NOT_PROVEN_FOR_HARD_GATE`: F9 is the only rung that re-estimates the `g` pipeline, a strictly
larger perturbation than the conservatism proof covers, and its bias direction is unproven in
*either* direction. **Its PASS/FAIL may not be cited as evidence of validated robustness.** A
module-level import guard raises if F9 ever enters the hard set.

## 5. Decision rule

The benchmark passes iff **all three** hold:

```
G1 wilson_lower_95(competent, 164)  >= 0.70
G2 wilson_lower_95(successes, 144)  >= 0.70
G3 wilson_upper_95(violations, 36)  <= 0.15
```

There is no existence test, no composite score, and no substitution of an acceptance count for an
endpoint rate. Wilson intervals for the primary endpoints use `z = 1.96`.

## 6. Execution and sealing

Held-out executed under commit `8d87143d4280602323aa33ee0b5481aaef0fb4a8` with a clean tree. All 240
cases completed; **zero searches ended in execution failure** (7,136 seeds returned candidates, 64
returned none). One case ran 24,216 s against a 117 s median — disclosed as an operational anomaly,
carrying a single provenance entry with no re-run, in a family that carries no primary endpoint and
that terminated at A1 adequacy, so it is scientifically inert.

The partition was sealed before any analysis: a receipt records the SHA-256 of all 482 raw files
(240 case records, 240 seed-record files, 1 provenance log, 1 execution manifest). An analysis
boundary guard refuses to run on an unsealed or modified run.

Under A3.5 §8.2, if **any one** of a case's 30 seeds fails, the whole case is UNEVALUABLE — there is
no replacement seed, no 29/30 denominator, and no retry. Execution-failure-poisoned counts are a
mandatory per-endpoint disclosure.

## 7. Analysis

The analysis layer **invokes** the frozen scorers rather than reimplementing them. Endpoint
populations are constructed from `registry.endpoint_applies_to_variant` and asserted against
`registry.endpoint_case_count` before any scoring, and the frozen scorers' own length assertions
(164 / 144 / 36) are allowed to fire.

Before scoring, the full acceptance predicate was re-evaluated for all 240 cases from their own
stored inputs and compared against the sealed verdicts: **0 disagreements**, establishing that the
raw records are faithful to the frozen contract.

Three independent checks were applied:

1. **Independent recomputation** — a module that shares no object with the primary analyzer and
   re-derives every rule from the frozen contract text, including its own ordered predicate, its own
   population construction by a different traversal, and its own Wilson arithmetic. Structural
   independence is enforced by an import-time guard that is itself tested.
2. **Seven hostile lenses** — provenance, populations, G1, G2, G3, gates, and governance. Each
   reconstructs what it audits rather than asserting it. Thirteen mutation tests demonstrate that
   each lens can actually fail.
3. **Differential test against the superseded rules** — the previously reported rules were
   re-implemented and run on the identical evidence, confirming they reproduce their documented
   numbers and that the restored analysis differs from every one of them.

### 7.1 G1 recovery without search

The sealed record schema persists no G1 observable. Because G1's inputs are search-independent, all
164 G1-applicable cases were rescored through `materialize_case → fit_case_scalars → score_case_g1`
with **zero searches**; the symbolic engine was never imported, and this is asserted programmatically
rather than claimed.

Content identity was **verified, not assumed**. A1 adequacy is itself search-independent, so it was
re-derived from each regenerated case and compared against that case's sealed
`a1_case_adequacy_status`: **164 of 164 matched, 0 mismatches.** A regenerated case differing in any
way A1 can see would have surfaced as a mismatch.
