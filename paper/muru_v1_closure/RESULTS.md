# MURU ConjectureLab v1 — Results

Held-out partition, 240 cases, 7,200 searches, zero execution failures. All figures generated by
`scripts/build_muru_v1_manuscript_tables.py`; tables in `tables/`, figure data in `figure_data/`.

## 1. Headline

**All three primary endpoints fail, independently and by wide margins.**

| Endpoint | Denominator | Result | Wilson 95% | Gate | Margin | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** scalar competence | 164 | 67 competent (40.9%) | lower **0.336233** | ≥ 0.70 | −0.364 | **FAIL** |
| **G2** family recovery | 144 | 4 successes (2.8%) | lower **0.010854** | ≥ 0.70 | −0.689 | **FAIL** |
| **G3** structural safety | 36 | 26 violations (72.2%) | upper **0.841518** | ≤ 0.15 | +0.692 | **FAIL** |

No single correction changes any verdict. The decision rule — the conjunction of the three Wilson
gates — returns `false`, and would return `false` under any one of the three alone.

Gate summary: Gate 7 reached by 27 cases, passed by 26. Gate 8 reached by 26, passed by 25.
`STRUCTURAL_ACCEPTED` 25 of 240 — an acceptance count, not an endpoint rate.

## 2. G1 — the failure is adequacy, not estimation

G1's exact observables were recovered with zero searches (Methods §7.1), with content identity
verified on 164 of 164 cases. The stratification is the result:

| Stratum | Cases | `g_spearman ≥ 0.80` | `trajectory_mae ≤ 0.80·baseline` | both | **competent** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 established (`M0_NOT_REJECTED`) | 67 | **67** | **67** | **67** | **67** |
| A1 not established (`BOUNDARY_LIMITED`) | 97 | 94 | 89 | 87 | **0** |
| all | 164 | 161 | 156 | 154 | **67** |

**Every case whose A1 adequacy was established passed both continuous conjuncts — 67 of 67.** Zero
cases failed on `g_spearman` alone; zero failed on `trajectory_mae` alone; zero recorded a contract
failure.

The scalar estimator is not the problem. Across all 164 cases `g_spearman` has median 0.9806 (min
0.6725, max 1.0000) against a 0.80 gate, and the trajectory skill ratio has median 0.3622 (min
0.0013, max 0.9712) against a 0.80 gate — the model beats the per-energy-mean baseline by roughly
a factor of three at the median.

**G1 fails because the A1 adequacy ladder returned `BOUNDARY_LIMITED` for 97 of 164 cases (59%), and
`BOUNDARY_LIMITED` is UNEVALUABLE under the frozen contract.**

> **Diagnostic counterfactual, not an endpoint.** Had adequacy been established across the
> population, the two continuous conjuncts alone would have admitted 154 of 164. This number
> characterises the failure mechanism. It is **not** a G1 result, may not be reported as a rate, and
> enters no gate. The endpoint is 67/164.

## 3. G2 — a genuine symbolic-recovery failure

4 successes from 144: 103 outright failures and 37 unevaluable, the unevaluable remaining in the
fixed denominator. Successes are `PB|held_out|F01|r002`, `PB|held_out|F01|r005`,
`PB|held_out|F11|r010`, `PB|held_out|F12|r000` — F01 ×2, F11 ×1, F12 ×1. Eight of the twelve
G2-carrying families recorded **no** success at all.

Unlike G1 and G3, this endpoint fails on its own terms rather than through unevaluability: 103 of
the 144 cases produced a resolvable support and family that simply did not match the planted truth.
Recovering the right variables with the wrong functional form does not count, and the conjunction is
where most candidates were lost.

Notably, the single success in F01 — the noiseless family, the easiest in the benchmark — occurred
in only 2 of 12 replicates. Symbolic family recovery under this grammar, budget and selection rule
did not succeed even where the signal is clean.

## 4. G3 — no unsafe acceptance, but a conservative failure

26 violations from 36. The breakdown is the finding:

| Acceptance status | Cases | G3 event |
|---|---|---|
| `UNEVALUABLE` | **26** | VIOLATION |
| `REJECTED_UNSTABLE` | 4 | SAFE |
| `REJECTED_BELOW_NULL` | 3 | SAFE |
| `REJECTED_FALSIFICATION` | 1 | SAFE |
| `STRUCTURAL_ACCEPTED` | 2 | SAFE — both mass-only, permitted |

**There are zero `UNSAFE` events.** The pipeline never accepted a forbidden structure in a safety
family. Both safety-family cases it did accept carried mass-only support, which their variants
permit.

Every one of the 26 violations is an UNEVALUABLE case counted as a violation by the frozen
conservative rule. By variant: F20A/F20B/F20C 4/4 each, F19A 4/4, F07 8/12, F19B 1/4, F19C 1/4.

The scientific reading requires care in both directions. The endpoint fails, and it fails
legitimately: the contract was frozen prospectively to treat unevaluability as a safety violation,
on the grounds that a system that cannot evaluate a null world has not demonstrated it would refuse
one. But the failure mode is *inability to reach a verdict*, not *reaching a wrong verdict*. On the
ten safety cases the pipeline could evaluate, it was safe ten times out of ten.

Nearly two-thirds of the partition never reached the second gate. Of the 86 cases that cleared A1,

The two structural gates that received the most design attention, Gate 7 and Gate 8, rejected one
case each. **The benchmark's outcome was determined almost entirely at A1 adequacy and at seed
stability, upstream of every gate the falsification framework was built to enforce.**

## 6. Gates

### 6.1 Gate 7 — the ceiling comparison

27 cases reached it; 26 passed, 1 failed (`PB|held_out|F02|r004`).

**All 26 passed via `ceiling_pass` (`ceiling_fraction ≥ 0.80`). Zero passed via the waiver branch.**
The waiver — the low-ceiling regime into which F5's floor was folded — was never entered. The A3.5
Gate-7 amendment is therefore **outcome-invariant on this partition as a matter of fact**: the
amended and pre-amendment predicates produce identical results on this evidence.

### 6.2 Gate 8 — hard falsification

26 cases reached it; 25 passed, 1 failed (`PB|held_out|F07|r005`).

| Rung | PASS | FAIL |
|---|---|---|
| F1_REPRODUCIBILITY | 26 | 0 |
| F4_COMPOUND_HOLDOUT | 26 | 0 |
| F7_INFLUENCE_DROP | 26 | 0 |

The sole Gate-8 rejection is driven entirely by `F10_NEGATIVE_CONTROL`. Three of the four hard rungs
never rejected anything. `F5_SCAFFOLD_HOLDOUT` appears in **zero** of the 240 records, confirming its
supersession operationally as well as in the enum.

### 6.3 Structural acceptance

25 of 240 cases passed all eight gates: F18 ×7, F10 ×6, F09 ×4, F04 ×2, and one each from F02, F05,
F07, F08, F12, F19. This is an acceptance count and carries no frozen denominator.

## 8. Mandatory disclosures

**Execution failures.** Zero. Across all 7,200 searches: 7,136 `COMPLETED_WITH_CANDIDATES`, 64
`COMPLETED_NO_CANDIDATE`, **0 `EXECUTION_FAILURE`**. Execution-failure-poisoned cases: 0 of 164 for
G1, 0 of 144 for G2, 0 of 36 for G3. **No endpoint is contaminated by execution failure**, so every
UNEVALUABLE outcome reported above is a scientific result, not an infrastructure artifact.

**Operational anomaly.** One case (`PB|held_out|F14|r008`) ran 24,216 s against a 117 s median. It
carries exactly one provenance entry with no re-run, the same run commit and digests as all others,
and all 30 seeds completed. Family F14 carries no primary endpoint, and the case is UNEVALUABLE at
A1 adequacy, so it reached neither Gate 7 nor Gate 8 and contributes to no endpoint.

**Analysis-contract failure and repair.** The originally reported Held-out analysis was defective in
a way that inverted the verdict, reporting `decision_passed: true` from an analyzer that scored every
endpoint over a 240 denominator, substituted `candidate_test_r2` for G1, relaxed G2's conjunction to
a disjunction, inverted G3's direction, conflated Gate 7 with structural acceptance, and composed
Gate 8 as "Gate 7 AND G1". Every deviation ran in the permissive direction. The analysis was
reconstructed against the frozen contract, cross-checked by a structurally independent
recomputation and seven hostile lenses, and reproduces the sealed pre-repair forensic result exactly
on all eight quantities that result determined. **No search was rerun and no sealed evidence was
modified.** Full account: `audit/MURU_HELDOUT_RESTORED_ANALYSIS.md` and the supersession ledger.

## 9. What these results do and do not establish

**Established, for this synthetic population and this frozen configuration:**

- Symbolic family recovery failed comprehensively (4/144), including on noiseless cases.
- Scalar competence is high *conditional on adequacy being established* (67/67) and the endpoint
  nevertheless fails, because adequacy was established for only 41% of the population.
- The safety endpoint fails on unevaluability, with zero observed unsafe acceptances.
- The dominant attrition is upstream of every falsification gate: A1 adequacy (154 cases) and seed
  stability (43 cases) together account for 82% of the partition.

**Not established, and not claimed:**

- Nothing about real CID chemistry, real spectra, or any measured analyte. Every case is synthetic.
- Nothing about performance under a different grammar, search budget, selection rule, adequacy
  ladder or calibration. All were frozen before execution and none was varied.
- Nothing about the Challenge partition, which has not been opened, or the real-data Confirmation
  partition, which remains sealed.
- No robustness claim from F9, which is explicitly barred from carrying one.
