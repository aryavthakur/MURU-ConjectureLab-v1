# NHSJS format validation

This is an internal, AI-assisted manuscript package formatted against the current official NHSJS materials. It has not been submitted.

## Official requirements checked

Reviewed the [NHSJS Submission Guidelines](https://nhsjs.com/submission-guidelines/) and downloaded both official Word templates on 3 November 2026. The webpage specifies the section order Title; Authors and affiliations; Abstract; Introduction; Methods; Results; Discussion; Acknowledgments; References; a 200–250-word abstract; Standard-citation manuscripts no longer than 20 pages, 12-point type and single spacing; superscript numerical citations before punctuation; references numbered by first appearance; and online citations as complete references within double parentheses, with a preceding space and superscript commas between multiple references. The actual standard and online template files were used as the source for document styles and section geometry. The templates and webpage do not conflict on these requirements.

Template SHA-256: Standard `387d533c8a419b3c7b33510300be235b821c5c4ce676f9ed305c753bb06b65f0`; Online `1c5e820f7ff6b1881037daca3415d91e0ed4c0ba703caaffaddd01ab4c09c5ee`. The template binaries were inspected as generation inputs and are excluded from the deliverable archive; the builder retrieves the current official files from these URLs when run. Their verified hashes are recorded above.

The templates use US Letter portrait pages with 1-inch margins; Normal is Times New Roman 12 pt, single-spaced; NHSJS Title is 18 pt bold; NHSJS Section is 14 pt bold; NHSJS Subsection is bold italic. The submitted documents retain the template’s page geometry and named style ladder. Body sections follow the required order. The manuscript’s Conclusion and study limitations are included as Discussion subsections. The anonymous standard file omits the author and acknowledgments/declarations block.
