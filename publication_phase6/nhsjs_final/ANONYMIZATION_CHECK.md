# Anonymous manuscript check

The standard review DOCX has no author line, affiliation, acknowledgments, or declarations block. Core Word properties for author, last editor, title, subject, keywords, and comments are blank. Header/footer parts are absent; Word comments and tracked-change insert/delete markup were not found. No local `/Users/...` paths, email addresses, or full `Aryav Thakur` name occur in package XML. The official title-based DOCX and PDF are byte-identical to the anonymous standard manuscript and PDF. PDF Info title, author, subject, and keywords are blank; its XMP metadata has no author identity or local path. The generic creator field identifies Writer only.

Scientific self-citations and repository URLs were deliberately retained because the owner instructed that provenance and references not be removed merely because they identify project records. Therefore the file is anonymized in its direct author metadata, but complete reviewer blindness cannot be guaranteed; a reviewer may infer ownership from project citations and the public repository URL.
