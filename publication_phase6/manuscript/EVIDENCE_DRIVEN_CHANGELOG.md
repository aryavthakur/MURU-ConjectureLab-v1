The manuscript is being narrowed to distinguish the converted-file annotation from the original acquisition setting: the native Xcalibur method, the conversion provenance, and exact RAW-to-mzML hash linkage were not recovered. It explicitly says the frozen adapter did not convert eV into NCE and cannot establish physical transfer to the WUR/IQ-X coordinate.

The external numerical analysis, outcome population, model artifacts, metrics, confidence interval, practical-improvement criterion, tail findings, and frozen verdict are unchanged. The historical [0.860, 0.918] development max-T interval remains unreproduced.
