# Phase 6 data-rights assessment

This technical distribution assessment is not legal advice. It distinguishes internal retention, sharing with a named reviewer, and public redistribution.

- MSnLib v5 converted mzML and positive RAW Zenodo records identify CC BY 4.0 terms. Phase 6 cites DOI/version and does not package RAW, mzML, or unredacted `.meth` payloads. Method summaries and scan-metadata summaries are derived from these records; the package does not declare unrestricted public redistribution without attribution and derivative-scope review.
- The WUR source record is restricted. WUR raw data, WUR-derived record-level data, and candidate weights/checkpoints with restricted or mixed training provenance are excluded from the external review package.
- Compound-associated MSnLib outcome/error tables are derived data and remain excluded from the external package pending attribution and sharing-scope review.
- Project-authored documents and code are included in the internal package. Owner confirmation and a sharing-scope/license decision are required before the external review bundle is sent or project materials are publicly posted.
- The external review package contains the manuscript and technical review briefs, not raw spectra, model files, or compound-level error tables. It is labeled PREPARED - NOT CLEARED TO SEND.

See `../rights/EXACT_FILE_RIGHTS_MATRIX.csv`, `../rights/THIRD_PARTY_PERMISSIONS_REGISTER.md`, and `../rights/EXTERNAL_REVIEW_DISTRIBUTION_DECISION.md`.
