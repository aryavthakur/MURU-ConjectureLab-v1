## Verified official requirements

- Research articles require an abstract, introduction, four to five main-text figures or tables, and section subheadings; additional methods, figures, and tables may be placed in online supporting material. [Submission Types](https://nhsjs.com/submit-your-work/submission-types/)
- The submission guidelines specify an abstract of 200–250 words, sections in the order title; authors/affiliations; abstract; introduction; methods; results; discussion; acknowledgments; references, and a standard-citation manuscript maximum of 20 pages including figures and tables, at 12-point, single-spaced formatting. They request standard and online-citation Word versions (the page also describes a LaTeX route). [Submission Guidelines](https://nhsjs.com/submission-guidelines/)
- Standard citations are numbered, sequential, and superscripted before punctuation. Online publication citations use full citation text in double parentheses. Reference formatting includes complete author lists and sentence-case titles. Templates and a CSL are linked on the official page. [Submission Guidelines](https://nhsjs.com/submission-guidelines/)
- NHSJS currently requires that submitted prose be independently composed by the author. Its AI policy allows research support such as code, analysis, and literature search but prohibits AI drafting, paraphrasing, expanding, or rewriting submission prose. AI use must be disclosed at the end of a submission, after acknowledgments and before references; AI cannot be an author. [AI Usage Policy](https://nhsjs.com/ai-usage-policy/)
- The journal states that research articles receive professional peer review. [Submission Types](https://nhsjs.com/submit-your-work/submission-types/)
