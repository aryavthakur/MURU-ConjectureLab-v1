## Model adequacy came before model prestige

The master plan's §9.2 formulation was treated as a candidate, not as
truth, and was checked before the likelihood was frozen.

| Diagnostic | Value | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| mu range | [0.0954, **1.0000008**] | logit and Beta are **undefined** at the upper end |
| rows with mu > 1 | **7** | caused by the ~9 ppm recalibration effect Phase 1 recorded as D1 |
| logit(mu) linear in NCE | median R² 0.824, 69.8% below 0.9 | the two-parameter form does not describe these trajectories |
| logit(mu) linear in log NCE | median R² 0.926, 40.2% below 0.9 | better, still inadequate |
| residual SD ratio across energies | 1.379 | heteroskedasticity mild |
| Spearman(abs residual, fitted) | 0.1298 | no strong mean-variance coupling |

**Decisions taken on that evidence**, recorded as `DEVIATIONS.md` D7 and D8:
mu is modelled on the **natural scale**, and energy is treated
**nonparametrically** (6-level factor or spline), never as a two-parameter
linear term. Clipping mu into (0,1) to rescue the logit was rejected: it
would silently edit measured values to suit a modelling convenience.

## Why not PyMC

Implementation: **statsmodels MixedLM (see DEVIATIONS D11 for why not PyMC)**.

A probabilistic programming framework was considered and deliberately not
used (Phase 2 instruction 14). mu has no censoring — that was survival
yield, which Phase 2 does not model as a continuous response; n is ample
(2610 observations, 439 compounds); and every interval reported
in Phase 2 comes from a compound-level bootstrap rather than a posterior.
Adding PyMC would have introduced sampler-convergence risk and a second
model definition without changing a single Phase 2 answer. The cost is
that intervals here are frequentist bootstrap intervals, not posterior
credible intervals.

## Descriptor-explained share of between-compound variance (K5 input)

The master plan's K5 threshold is a descriptor-driven between-compound
variance share of **0.20**. Reported both in-sample and cross-validated;
**only the cross-validated figure is used**, because an in-sample R² from
12 descriptors on a few hundred compounds is optimistic by construction.

**K5 variance-share verdict.** Tier A explains **0.4693** of the
