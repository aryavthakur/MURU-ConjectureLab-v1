# NHSJS future requirements snapshot

Checked 2026-11-03 against official NHSJS pages: [Submission Guidelines](https://nhsjs.com/submission-guidelines/) and [AI Usage Policy](https://nhsjs.com/ai-usage-policy/). This is a planning record only. No manuscript was submitted.

## Verified current requirements relevant to this draft

- Required order: title; authors and affiliations; abstract; introduction; methods; results; discussion; acknowledgments; references.
- Structured abstract has 200–250 words and covers background/objective, methods, results, conclusions, and keywords.
- Standard-citation manuscript is limited to 20 pages including figures and tables, 12-point, single-spaced.
- Word submissions require at least two versions: anonymized standard citation manuscript and online-citation manuscript with the complete citation in double parentheses. Online citation references must be consistent.
- References are numbered by first appearance; all author names are to be listed; the journal page specifies article title, journal, volume, pages, year, DOI, and full web reference information.
- The current AI policy states submitted sentences must be independently composed by the author and prohibits AI drafting, paraphrasing, expansion, or rewriting; it also requires disclosure of AI tools and uses. The present internal manuscript is AI-drafted, so it is not automatically eligible for submission under that policy. A future submission would require the human author to write compliant prose and re-evaluate all current policies.
- The official site describes NHSJS as a journal targeted to high-school students / publishing original research by high-school students. Aryav’s current eligibility and project authorship declaration have not been independently confirmed here.
- Supporting materials, ethics, source data, and author declarations must be checked against the specific submission instructions and any application/form version then in effect.
