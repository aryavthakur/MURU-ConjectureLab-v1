It diagnoses the Phase 3 version before Phase 4 changes. Severity reflects the risk to scientific interpretation or reproducibility.

## Overall assessment

The Phase 3 manuscript already contains a complete argument, the correct primary population and external point estimates, the failed 5% threshold, the adverse least-similar quartile, and the unresolved energy-interface result. It is not ready to circulate unchanged because the comparator-method description is factually wrong, the ridge equation and preprocessing description are incomplete, and the Phase 2 provenance statements are stale. These issues are correctable from preserved source material. The external RMSE result remains a small, endpoint-specific transfer result with no demonstrated tail advantage; it cannot support a general superiority claim.

## Phase 4 evidence recovery update

The diagnostic above reflects the Phase 3 manuscript before its revisions. During Phase 4, the older Phase 2 audit worktree was recovered. It contains the Phase 2 CI provenance report, energy adjudication, census verifier and four-frame census evidence, and the actual 20-row Validex crosswalk. It does not recover the original development max-T script, seed, serialized resamples or selection ledger; that interval remains unreproduced. The census verifier was rerun and its zero-membership-difference finding was confirmed only for the four recorded hashed frames.
