# Phase 4 executive summary

Phase 4 audited and revised the complete Phase 3 manuscript against frozen source code, data summaries, historical protocols, recovered Phase 2 audit records, and a fresh extraction of the reviewer archive. It corrected a substantive description of comparator scoring (full-spectrum predicted peaks were reduced to the shared scalar μ endpoint before P1), completed the Ridge objective and preprocessing descriptions, separated the WUR and MSnLib precursor denominator sources, narrowed independence/energy-transfer wording, and disclosed the full practical and adverse-tail decision rule.

The frozen result remains 1,789 scored compounds across 1,687 scaffold groups; MURU P1 0.1296115107, reference P1 0.1335276112, ratio 0.9706719801 (95% scaffold bootstrap 0.9523611880–0.9892672062), relative reduction 2.9328%. The 5% practical target failed; tail benefit was not demonstrated and Q1 ratio was 1.0083.

Five figures and a 5-page supplement were regenerated. The manuscript is 13 pages and abstract 248 words; bibliography includes 27 entries. Three simulated reviewers found no remaining correctable major error after the second revision. Physical energy calibration, historical max-T provenance, raw-data processing/training reproduction, and human declarations remain open.
