# Final research readiness

## Status

The Phase 4 internal manuscript and supporting package are complete as a carefully bounded research draft and ready for human scientific review. This is not a journal acceptance or eligibility determination. No submission has been made.

## Evidence-supported finding

From 1,794 measured MSnLib compounds, 5 fell outside model support and 1,789 compounds in 1,687 scaffold groups were scored. Candidate/reference pooled P1 RMSE ratio was 0.9706719801 (95% whole-scaffold bootstrap interval 0.9523611880–0.9892672062), a 2.9328% relative RMSE reduction. The interval excludes 1 but lies above the prespecified 0.95 practical target. No tail-risk improvement was demonstrated; the least-similar quartile ratio was 1.0083.

## Remaining limitations

1. Collision-energy Design A remains `INTERFACE_UNRESOLVED`; the affine input map is computational and not physically calibrated.
2. The historical development max-T interval [0.860, 0.918] remains unreproduced because original script, seed, resamples, and selection ledger are missing.
3. Raw mzML processing and training are not reproduced from the reviewer archive.
4. Candidate/reference differences do not isolate the Morgan feature contribution.
5. A previously exposed sample was excluded and replaced; the replacement sample has now been analyzed and exposed.
6. A human author must confirm contribution, competing-interest, data-rights and any future submission declarations.
